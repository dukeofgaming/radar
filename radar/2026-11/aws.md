AWS showed up twice: as the EKS target of the 2021 interview deploy, and as the real deploy of a small API in 2024. [flexion-unitconverter](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/flexion-unitconverter) is the interview unit converter. [ipfs-metadata](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ipfs-metadata) is the Go API, with Terraform for ECS, RDS, S3 state, and a load balancer. Google Cloud remains the platform he used on the job.
