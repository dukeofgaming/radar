Component-driven development means building the interface as small pieces with their own stories and tests. [ab-ui](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-ui) is that library: age check, buttons, inputs, and data tables, developed in Storybook. [hotto-test](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/hotto-test), the survey app, wrote the argument down before the library existed.
