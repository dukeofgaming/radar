Dev Containers are the supported setup for the active repositories, so a new checkout opens with the right toolchain. [ab-ui](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-ui) publishes the image it uses. [scriptorium](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/scriptorium/pull/5), the Markdown sync CLI, and [detent](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/detent/tree/cucumber), the BPMN converter, both open in one.
