Go is one service, not a daily language. [ipfs-metadata](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ipfs-metadata) is a small API, Gin and sqlx, that pulls NFT metadata from IPFS into Postgres and ships it to AWS. He added the Docker image and the Terraform on top of an earlier scraper.
