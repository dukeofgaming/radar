Short architecture decision records are how choices get written down before the code drifts. The first one, at Flexion, argued for Helm over Terraform for Kubernetes service installs. [hotto-test](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/hotto-test), the survey app, has a numbered set, and [ab-ui](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-ui) records why it chose React, Vite, and Storybook.
