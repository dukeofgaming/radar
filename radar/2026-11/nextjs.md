Next.js is the framework for the small sites built on the component library in 2025. [ab-app](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-app) is the starter. [ab-app-brand-site](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-app-brand-site) is a multi-brand marketing site on that starter. [ab-app-brands](https://github.com/ab-internal/ab-app-brands) is a fork of the starter for editing brand records, including a theme switcher. A private restaurant site, buddys, is the one deployed on Vercel.
