Pandoc was a candidate for turning the Markdown design notes into other formats, and for round-tripping them. It stayed an evaluation. The tool he built for that job is [scriptorium](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/scriptorium/pull/7), which copies the Markdown itself to Jira, Confluence, Google Docs, and GitHub.
