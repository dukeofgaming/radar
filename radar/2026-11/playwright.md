Playwright is the browser test runner, first on the survey app and then on the component library. [hotto-test](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/hotto-test) is the survey app. [ab-ui](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-ui) pairs Storybook with Playwright. A private spend dashboard and a private bibliography app use it too, the latter through playwright-bdd.
