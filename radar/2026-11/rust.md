Rust is the language of the newer command-line tools, started in 2023 and used in earnest this year. [socioscope](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/socioscope/tree/rust-port) is the audio transcriber, and the rust-port branch repeats it with whisper-rs and sherpa-onnx. [scriptorium](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/scriptorium/pull/7) keeps a Markdown file in sync with Jira, Confluence, Google Docs, and GitHub. [detent](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/detent/tree/cucumber) converts BPMN process diagrams to Markdown and back. A private spend ledger and a private knowledge notebook are Rust as well.
