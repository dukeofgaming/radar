Screaming architecture means the folders are named for the business, and clean architecture means the database sits behind an interface. [hotto-test](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/hotto-test), the survey app, writes both down and keeps MySQL behind a gateway. The private org-map app splits the same way: domain, scene, and persistence.
