TypeScript is the language of the typed interfaces, starting with the Flexion unemployment-insurance UI and continuing in the design system. [ab-ui](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-ui) is the React component library, and [ab-app](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-app) is the Next.js starter that consumes it. The private desktop apps use it too.
