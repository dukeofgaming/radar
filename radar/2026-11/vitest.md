Vitest is the unit-test runner for the TypeScript projects. [ab-ui](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/ab-ui) uses it with Testing Library, and the private desktop apps use it as well. [angular-ds](https://github.com/dukeofgaming/angular-ds), an Angular hello-world, also runs Vitest.
